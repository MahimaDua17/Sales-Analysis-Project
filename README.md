This ecommerce sales dashboard provides insights into sales performance across different dimensions such as state, customer, category, payment mode, and time. The data showcases a total sales amount of ₹438K, 5615 units sold, ₹37K in profit, and an average order value (AOV) of ₹121K.
🔍 Key Findings:
1. Top Performing States by Sales Amount:
Maharashtra leads with the highest sales.

Followed by Madhya Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh. 👉 Maharashtra is the most significant contributor to revenue.

2. Sales Quantity by Product Category:
Clothing dominates with 63% of total quantity sold.

Followed by Electronics (21%) and Furniture (17%). 👉 Clothing is the top-selling category, indicating high customer preference.

3. Preferred Payment Modes (by Quantity):
Cash on Delivery (COD) is most used (44%).

Followed by UPI (21%), Debit Card (13%), and Credit Card (12%). 👉 COD remains the most preferred method, signaling trust and convenience factors.

4. Top Customers by Sales Amount:
Harivansh is the highest spending customer, followed by Madhav and Madan Mohan. 👉 These customers may be ideal for loyalty programs or targeted offers.

5. Profit by Sub-Category:
Printers bring the highest profit, followed by Bookcases and Sarees. 👉 Tech and home office categories seem profitable and worth promoting.

6. Monthly Profit Trend:
Highest profit in December.

Sharp drop observed in May, followed by fluctuations. 👉 Strong year-end sales, possible festive or holiday season spike; performance dips mid-year.

✅ Recommendations:
Boost Clothing and Electronics Inventory to meet high demand.

Run promotions during low-profit months like May to stabilize monthly revenue.

Encourage digital payments by offering discounts or cashback to reduce COD dependence.

Target high-value customers (like Harivansh and Madhav) with exclusive offers.

Increase focus on high-profit sub-categories like Printers and Bookcases with marketing campaigns.
